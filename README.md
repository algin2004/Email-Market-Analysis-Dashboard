# Email Market Analysis Dashboard

This repository contains a **Power BI dashboard** that provides insights into email marketing data, including user status, demographics, location, and engagement trends.

---

## Folder Structure

- **data/**  
  Contains the dataset(s) used for creating the dashboard.

- **powerbi/**  
  Contains the Power BI report file(s) (`.pbix`).

- **reports/**  
  Contains screenshots and exported images of the dashboard, such as `Screenshot (31).png`.

---

## Dashboard Overview

The **Email Market Analysis Dashboard** provides a comprehensive overview of email marketing metrics. Key visualizations include:

- **Count of Status**: Total active and inactive users.
- **Count of Gender**: Distribution of male, female, and other users.
- **Count of States & Cities**: Distribution of users by state and city.
- **Count of Marital Status**: Segmentation based on marital status.
- **Count of Living Status**: Distribution of users by living arrangements.
- **Enrollment Time Analysis**: Visual representation of enrollment times.
- **Yearly Trends**: Increment and decrement of user engagement over years.

The dashboard helps identify trends, demographic patterns, and engagement insights for effective email marketing strategy.
